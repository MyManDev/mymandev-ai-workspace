# Workspaces and projects

A workspace holds people and projects. You can belong to several workspaces. The sidebar lists them once this device is signed in, and lists the projects of the selected workspace below them.

## Create a workspace

- If you have no workspace yet, the "Home" screen shows "Create a workspace". Enter a name and choose "Create".
- Otherwise, choose "New workspace" in the sidebar, below your workspaces. Enter a name and choose "Create".

You become the owner of the new workspace. Its people see you under your account's name. An owner cannot leave a workspace without handing the ownership over first (see [People and roles](people-and-roles.md)).

A name has at most 100 characters. You can create at most 10 workspaces in 24 hours.

## Create a project

Select a workspace, then choose "New project" in the sidebar, below its projects. Enter a name and choose "Create".

- Every member of the workspace may create a project, whatever their role.
- Everyone in the workspace sees the project.
- Two projects of a workspace cannot have the same name, whatever the case of the letters.
- A name has at most 100 characters. A workspace has at most 100 projects, and you can create at most 20 projects in 24 hours.

## Open what you created

- A new workspace is usually selected at once. When it is not, for example because chat messages still wait to be sent, the screen says "The workspace was created." and offers "Open this workspace".
- A new project shows in the list of projects once the server reports it. Then the screen says "The project was created." and offers "Open this project".

You can also select a workspace or open a project in the sidebar at any time. If chat messages still wait to be sent, the application asks first whether to keep them or to discard them and switch.
