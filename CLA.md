## Notes for legal review

Remove this section before the Agreement takes effect.

1. Written form: does § 40 Abs. 1 UrhG (contracts on future works) or § 31a UrhG (unknown types of use) apply? Acceptance by a GitHub comment is at most text form. Section 2.1(c) grants the licenses for each Contribution when it is Submitted, and Section 6.7 limits each version to Contributions Submitted while it applies; confirm whether that is enough, or whether only the signed-document way of accepting in Section 7 should remain.
2. Remuneration: do §§ 32 and 32a UrhG apply to a royalty-free grant to one company that allows commercial relicensing? The exception in § 32 Abs. 3 Satz 3 UrhG covers a free, simple right of use for everyone, which this grant is not.
3. Scope: is the definition of "Material" together with Section 2.1(b) broad enough for separately licensed commercial editions and for the cloud service? Is the purpose statement sufficient under § 31 Abs. 5 UrhG?
4. Standard terms: does control of standard business terms (§§ 305 ff. BGB) affect any clause? Unclear terms are read against the drafter (§ 305c Abs. 2 BGB), and a term that is not clear and understandable can be unfair (§ 307 Abs. 1 Satz 2 BGB).
5. Moral rights: is Section 2.4 effective under German law and under the laws of other countries contributors may live in?
6. Parties and identity: is a GitHub account enough to identify the contributor, or are a real name and address needed? Is the consent procedure for minors in Section 3(d) enough under §§ 106 to 108 BGB and for contributors in other countries?
7. Employees: how does § 69b UrhG interact with Sections 3(b), 3(c) and 6.3 for the core team and for contributors employed elsewhere? Please also draft the employer authorization that Section 3(c) relies on, adapted from the Harmony Entity Contributor License Agreement (HA-CLA-E) v1.0.
8. Enforcement: should We be authorized to enforce the license of the Material against infringers in Contributions on the contributor's behalf?
9. Data protection: confirm the legal bases, the retention period, the safeguard for the transfer to the United States, and the processing terms GitHub offers to an organization on its free plan. Entries in the public register cannot be erased from the repository history; confirm that a minimal public entry (numeric user ID and version) is acceptable before the first entry is committed.
10. Condition or obligation: should the promise in Section 2.3 be a condition of the licenses in Sections 2.1 and 2.2 (§ 158 BGB) or a contractual obligation? Does the last paragraph of Section 2.3 protect licenses and sublicenses already granted? Is the fallback to another license approved by the Open Source Initiative acceptable?
11. Output of software tools: is code that a contributor produces with software tools, directs and reviews protected under §§ 2 Abs. 2 and 69a Abs. 3 UrhG? Are the confirmations in Section 3(f) accurate for contributors, including the core team, who work this way? How does this affect what a commercial license can convey?
12. Third-party code: how do third-party code included under Section 3(e) and the licenses of dependencies affect commercial licensing? Is the list of allowed licenses in CONTRIBUTING.md sufficient?
13. Replacement clause: is Section 6.6 effective in standard terms, given that § 306 Abs. 2 BGB makes the statutory rules apply in place of an ineffective term?
14. Consumer contracts: do consumer-contract rules, including withdrawal rights (§§ 312 ff. and 355 BGB), apply when a private contributor accepts this Agreement online?
15. Venue: is Section 6.2 effective under Article 25 of the Brussels Ia Regulation and § 38 ZPO, for contributors inside and outside the EU?
16. Patent termination: does the last paragraph of Section 2.2 work as intended for sublicensees who hold commercial licenses?
